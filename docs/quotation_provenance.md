### Provenance corrections applied 2026-05-12

- `blake_imagination_01` — was previously sourced as the loose phrase "Imagination is the Human Existence itself" without a defensible plate citation. Corrected to the canonical *Jerusalem* plate 77 ("the real & eternal World…") wording.
- `blake_orc_01` — was previously misattributed to Boston's Angel. Corrected: the renunciation of obedience on plate 6 is **Orc's** speech, addressed to the Angel of Albion; Boston's Angel speaks the immediately preceding passage in a different rhetorical register.
- `blake_doors_01` — punctuation regularized to Erdman; added the continuation "For man has closed himself up…" for context.
- `blake_energy_01` — punctuation regularized to Erdman; the comma after "Body" is editorial.
- `blake_system_01` — added the continuation "I will not Reason & Compare…" and the contracted form "enslav'd" per Erdman.
- `blake_jerusalem_01` — extended to the full quatrain.
