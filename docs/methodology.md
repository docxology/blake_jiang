# Methodology

## Scope

The paper triangulates three voices on a single architectural question — *what kind of cognitive system can represent the full plurality of experience, and what happens when that system collapses into single vision?* The voices are:

1. **William Blake (1757–1827)** — through *The Marriage of Heaven and Hell*, *America: A Prophecy*, *The [First] Book of Urizen*, *The Four Zoas*, *Milton*, *Jerusalem*, and the 1802 letter to Thomas Butts.
2. **Professor Jiang Xueqin (b. 1976)** — through the *Predictive History* YouTube channel (2024-present), the *Endgame* #259 interview (Gita Wirjawan, March 2026), and reedited compilations.
3. **Daniel Ari Friedman (PhD)** — through the two Zenodo publications of early 2026: *The Doors of Perception are the Threshold of Prediction* (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18600041) and *Before Pragmatism Had a Name* (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18807971).

## Inclusions

- Primary-source quotation from all three voices, attributed with canonical citation (Blake) or timestamp (Jiang) or section (Friedman).
- The Active Inference / Free Energy Principle formal framework (Friston et al.).
- The American pragmatist tradition (Peirce, James, Mead, Dewey) at the points where it intersects Active Inference.
- Cognitive security as the political extension of the synthesis.

## Exclusions

- **Jiang's conspiratorial genealogies** (Illuminati, Sabbateans, etc.) are bracketed; their factual adequacy is not assessed here. §6 explicitly separates Jiang's defensible structural claims from this scaffolding.
- **Jiang's claims about Israel/Gaza** are documented by Wikipedia as antisemitic; they are noted in §6 as a reason for caution and are not invoked elsewhere.
- **Friston's full mathematical apparatus** is not derived; the paper uses Active Inference vocabulary at the level required for the convergence argument and points to Parr/Pezzulo/Friston (2022) for full treatment.
- **Hard claims that one tradition vindicates or completes another** — we follow Friedman's "Glass Bead Game" framing: synthetic juxtaposition rather than reduction.

## Epistemic Stance

The paper claims **structural convergence**, not equivalence. The three voices reach related architectural diagnoses through radically different methods: Blake's prophetic phenomenology, Jiang's speculative geopolitical commentary, Friedman's mathematical-philosophical synthesis. We argue that the convergence is meaningful precisely because the methods differ — if all three reached the same diagnostic, that diagnostic deserves attention.

## Verification

- Blake quotations are cross-referenced with the William Blake Archive and canonical editions (Erdman ed., *Complete Poetry and Prose of William Blake*, rev. ed. 1988).
- Jiang quotations carry verbatim timestamps from the transcript or interview; we preserve the lecture's spoken-English phrasing without smoothing.
- Friedman quotations are cited to the Zenodo DOI and the section / paragraph within.
- Numerical metrics in §6 (e.g., Newton's-Sleep ratios, cleansed-doors scores) are reproducible from `src/generative_model.py`.
