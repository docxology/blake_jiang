# Manuscript source

The 12 ordered sections are the source of *The Architecture of False Gods*.
[Config](config.yaml) contains the paper metadata and enabled formats.
[Bibliography](references.bib) contains the sources cited by the text.
The [preamble](preamble.md) declares LaTeX packages and environments.

## Reading order

1. [`00_abstract.md`](00_abstract.md) — what the paper argues, in ~500 words.
2. [`01_introduction.md`](01_introduction.md) — three voices, three registers, why triangulation is the method.
3. [`02_jiang_diagnosis.md`](02_jiang_diagnosis.md) — Jiang's AI critique, source-attributed with timestamps.
4. [`03_blake_architecture.md`](03_blake_architecture.md) — Blake's fourfold vision, Four Zoas, Newton's Sleep, doors of perception.
5. [`04_synthesis.md`](04_synthesis.md) — Active Inference scaffolding; structural correspondences between the three vocabularies.
6. [`05_twelve_nodes.md`](05_twelve_nodes.md) — node-by-node walkthrough of the twelve convergences.
7. [`06_cooperation_off_ramp.md`](06_cooperation_off_ramp.md) — multi-agent cooperation-off-ramp chapter.
8. [`07_critical_assessment.md`](07_critical_assessment.md) — separating Jiang's defensible claims from his conspiratorial framing.
9. [`08_implications.md`](08_implications.md) — AI alignment, cognitive security, Blake scholarship, public discourse.
10. [`09_conclusion.md`](09_conclusion.md) — closing synthesis.
11. [`10_glossary.md`](10_glossary.md) — glossary of cross-vocabulary terms.
12. [`99_references.md`](99_references.md) — references.

## Build

The section files are hand-authored. The seven light PNGs are generated
under the repository's `output/figures/` directory. Run the [native commands
and pinned renderer](../development.md) to regenerate the figures and build the
combined PDF and HTML. The [original May 12 PDF](../../blake_jiang_DAF_v1_05-12-2026.pdf)
is the published historical artifact.

## Tone

- Open scholarly publication register; not journalistic, not academic-jargon-heavy.
- Quotations attributed with timestamps (Jiang) or canonical source + plate (Blake).
- Honest about uncertainty; the paper claims convergence, not equivalence.
- Critical of Jiang's conspiratorial scaffolding while preserving the architectural insight.
